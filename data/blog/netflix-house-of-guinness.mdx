## Mohanlal Honored with India’s Highest Film Award

Mohanlal, often hailed as one of the greatest actors in Indian cinema, has been bestowed with the Dadasaheb Phalke Award, the country’s highest recognition for contributions to film. With a career spanning over four decades and more than 350 films, Mohanlal continues to mesmerize audiences with his versatility, making this award a fitting tribute to his enduring legacy.

---

## A Record-Breaking Achievement
At 65, Mohanlal becomes one of the few actors from the Malayalam industry to receive this honor, cementing his place not only in regional cinema but also in the larger fabric of Indian film history. His journey from commercial blockbusters to critically acclaimed roles reflects his unmatched ability to balance art and entertainment. This award recognizes not just his acting but his contribution to storytelling, culture, and the growth of Indian cinema on a global stage.


## Fans Celebrate the Big Win
At 65, Mohanlal proves that age is no barrier when passion and talent continue to inspire millions. His Dadasaheb Phalke Award win is not just a personal triumph but a proud moment for Malayalam cinema and Indian film as a whole. This historic recognition cements his status as an evergreen icon who is still going strong.
