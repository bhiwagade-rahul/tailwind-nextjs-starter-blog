## House of Guinness Release Date and Platform

The much-anticipated period drama House of Guinness was released on September 25, 2025, with all 8 episodes dropping at once on Netflix. The global release makes it accessible to audiences worldwide, allowing fans to binge-watch the entire series without waiting week after week.
---

## The Story of House of Guinness
The series explores the lives of the Guinness dynasty after the death of patriarch Sir Benjamin Guinness. His four children — Arthur, Edward, Anne, and Ben — find themselves entangled in a web of power struggles, inheritance conflicts, and family secrets. Set between Dublin and New York, the show captures the grandeur of the 19th century while diving into themes of ambition, loyalty, and betrayal.

## Why You Should Watch It
For fans of historical dramas like Peaky Blinders and The Crown, House of Guinness offers the perfect mix of family saga, period detail, and gripping storytelling. The costumes, sets, and cinematography recreate the richness of the era, while the performances bring the characters’ conflicts vividly to life. With Netflix backing the production, the show is set to reach a global audience and spark conversations about one of the most famous families in Irish history.
## Final Thoughts
With its global Netflix release, intriguing plotline, and powerful ensemble cast, House of Guinness is already being hailed as one of the standout dramas of 2025. Whether you’re a fan of history, family dramas, or gripping storytelling, this series deserves a spot on your watchlist.
