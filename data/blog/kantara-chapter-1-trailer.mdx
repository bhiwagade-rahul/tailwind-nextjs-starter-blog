## Release Date and Fan Craze

The film is scheduled for a grand release on October 2, 2025, and the response to advance bookings has been phenomenal. Within minutes of ticket sales opening, thousands of seats were sold across Karnataka. Early morning shows starting at 6:30 AM are already near housefull, proving just how strong the craze is for this prequel.

---

## Advance Booking Trends Across Languages
Advance sales are not just limited to Kannada. Dubbed versions in Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, and Malayalam are also witnessing strong demand. International pre-sales have shown remarkable numbers as well, confirming that Kantara: Chapter 1 is gearing up for a massive worldwide opening.


## Ticket Prices and Audience Response
Despite premium pricing in multiplexes, with tickets reaching higher-than-usual rates, fans are eager to grab their seats. The excitement shows that audiences are ready to invest in the cinematic experience of Rishab Shetty’s storytelling, blending folklore, action, and visual spectacle.


## Box Office Predictions
With advance bookings already crossing impressive figures, trade experts believe Kantara: Chapter 1 could deliver record-breaking opening day numbers. The film is expected to dominate the box office during its first weekend, continuing the legacy of the original Kantara, which became a cultural phenomenon.
