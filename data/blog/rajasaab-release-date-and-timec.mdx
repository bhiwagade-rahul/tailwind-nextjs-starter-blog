. Directed by Maruthi, the film has already created a storm on social media with its first looks and teaser updates. Now, the focus is on the official trailer release date and time, as fans eagerly wait to see more of Prabhas in this much-hyped entertainer.

According to reports, the trailer of The Raja Saab is expected to release on October 2, 2025, coinciding with the theatrical release of Kantara: Chapter 1. This strategic timing is expected to maximize buzz, with audiences in theatres getting the first big-screen glimpse of the film. While the teaser was dropped earlier on June 14, 2025 at 10:52 AM, the full trailer will showcase the scale, romance, and fantasy elements that Maruthi has promised.

With The Raja Saab scheduled for a grand theatrical release on December 5, 2025 (though some reports also suggest a possible January 9, 2026 shift), the trailer launch will play a crucial role in setting the tone for promotions. Fans are already speculating about the storyline, music, and Prabhas’ new avatar, making the trailer one of the most anticipated Telugu cinema events of the year.

The countdown has officially begun, and for millions of Prabhas fans, the Raja Saab trailer release is not just about a video drop — it’s about experiencing the magic of their favorite star returning to the big screen in a fresh and vibrant role.
